What "Matrika" Means

Matrika means the nurturing, motherly energy of healing — treating from the depth and vibration of natural sources, guided by the wisdom of homoeopathy.

Homoeopathic Physician

A Practice Built on Listening

Dr. Pujaa Scinde founded Matrika Homoeopathy with a simple belief: real healing begins when a doctor truly listens. Every consultation starts with a detailed case history — your physical symptoms, daily habits, emotional state, and family history — because homeopathy treats the person, not just the diagnosis.

She works with patients of all ages, from young children to seniors, treating both acute complaints and long-standing chronic conditions. Her approach blends classical homeopathic principles with an honest, modern bedside manner: clear communication, realistic expectations, and consistent follow-up.

Consultations are available both in-clinic and online, so patients can continue their care wherever they are.
