FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about online consultations at Matrika Homoeopathy.

What is Matrika Homoeopathy?

Matrika Homoeopathy provides online consultations with Dr. Pujaa Scinde (BHMS), with an emphasis on individualized assessment and patient-centred communication.

Do you offer physical consultations?

No. All consultations are conducted online. We do not currently offer in-clinic appointments.

How do I book an appointment?

You can contact us through our website or WhatsApp. We will share available appointment times, the consultation fee, and booking instructions.

Will I receive a prescription?

A digital prescription may be provided after the consultation if clinically appropriate and permitted under applicable professional requirements.

Do you provide medicines?

No. We do not sell, dispense, or ship medicines. Patients purchase prescribed medicines independently from a reputable pharmacy or authorized supplier.

Are medicines included in the consultation fee?

No. The consultation fee covers the consultation service only. Medicine costs and any delivery or pharmacy charges are separate.

Can I consult for chronic health concerns?

You may discuss long-standing concerns during an online consultation. Some conditions require an in-person examination, diagnostic tests, or specialist assessment.

Do you provide follow-ups?

Yes, follow-ups may be arranged depending on your needs. Applicable fees and appointment details will be communicated in advance.

Can I continue my existing treatment?

Please disclose all current medicines and treatments. Do not discontinue or change prescribed medication without consulting the healthcare professional responsible for your care.

Is my information confidential?

We handle consultation information in accordance with applicable privacy requirements. Please share sensitive medical information only through designated channels.

Can I consult from another city or state?

Eligibility depends on your location and applicable professional requirements. Please share your location when booking so that eligibility can be confirmed.

What if I have a medical emergency?

Online consultations are not intended for emergencies. Seek immediate medical attention for severe or urgent symptoms.

How soon will I see results?

Results vary according to the condition and individual circumstances. No specific outcome or recovery timeline can be guaranteed.

How can I contact you?

Use the WhatsApp button or contact form on our website for booking enquiries and consultation information.

Homoeopathic consultations are individualized. Treatment outcomes vary, and homoeopathy should not replace evidence-based medical care, particularly for serious or urgent conditions.
